# The listing the three counters share

One product, one description, one set of screenshots — so the shelves
cannot drift apart. Each counter's README says how to enter these into
its own console; the words and pictures come from here.

## Name

**QRME Studios**

## Short description

Your identity, your AI — synthetic profiles you create, train, embody
and even employ: chat, rooms, AR and VR, a marketplace, and companies
staffed one interview at a time.

## Long description

QRME is a synthetic-profiles platform. You create AI-driven profiles —
yourself, a consented other, or a fictional persona — and meet them
everywhere: chat that hears and remembers, rooms for up to nine, a
hands-free talk face, and a stage that renders the same room flat in
any browser, overlaid on your real environment in AR, or entered in VR
through your headset's own browser.

Profiles take bodies: watches, bands, rings, earbuds, glasses and
headsets pair from a published catalogue; robots bind from a shelf of
models sold in America; fixed screens stand where you put them — the
same identity across every embodiment.

Found a digital company in any industry, and the platform studies each
occupation, writes the interview, and staffs the roster one employee at
a time — then opens the storefront in the marketplace, and hands any
employee out as a QR code, a typed code, a link, or a file.

Every AI face and voice is marked as one. Refusals speak ten languages.
What a pair builds together is theirs to curate, export, and forget.

## Content rating, answered honestly

- AI-generated content: **yes** — the product's core; every AI face and
  generated utterance carries a mark that cannot be turned off.
- User-generated content: **yes** — posts, rooms, shops; moderated
  before display, with objections and takedowns on the record.
- User communication: **yes** — rooms and chat between account holders.
- Shares location: **no.**
- In-app purchases: plans and marketplace offerings.
- Age: accounts are age-verified at creation (18+ owners).
