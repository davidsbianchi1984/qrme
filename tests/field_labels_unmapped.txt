# `_FIELD_LABELS` covers the fields a person types into a form. These are the
# rest: enum members a control sets, ids a client fills in from the resource it
# is already looking at, and flags a switch owns. A 422 naming one of them is
# a client bug rather than something a person mistyped, which is why they are
# recorded rather than translated on spec.
#
# The identifier is the fallback on purpose. Inventing a word for a field
# nobody labels would be worse than the identifier a reader can at least match
# to the form in front of them.
#
