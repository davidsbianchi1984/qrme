# `Onboarding.tsx` calls `visitorLang()` three times, all on the links

# pre-session surface clean because it was only ever looking at one half.

#     asked     is the accountless screen localized
#     mattered  is the pre-session surface localized
#
# So: sign up, sign in, "at least 8 characters", "the passwords don't match
# yet", the whole email-verification and password-reset flow — English, on the
# first screen anybody in the world sees, in an app whose console speaks ten
# languages the moment you are through it.
#
# Not translated in this round on purpose. Forty-odd strings is its own round
# and a half-translated sign-up form is worse than an English one: it reads as
# broken software at the exact moment somebody is deciding whether to trust
# the thing with their email. The keys already exist in `l10n.ts` for the
# accountless screen and the same `fill` template machinery applies.
