**QRME v0.12.0** — **the specification, mined.** The filed patent
specification (App. 19/056,418, SYNTHETIC USER PROFILE MANAGEMENT) was
read end to end, and everything it describes that the apps did not yet
do was built in:

- **Blend a Profile** — a hybrid persona blended from several people:
  both grandparents at once, in the shares you choose, each lending the
  side of them you name. It says openly that it is a blend; rated
  profiles never blend.
- **What Would They Do** — real-time simulation of the represented
  person's likely decision and workflow, with confidence earned from
  real evidence (sources and remembered conversations), never from how
  sure the model sounds. Watermarked synthetic, owner-only.
- **Where You Are** — tell the profile where you are and the reply
  meets you there; the context is stored beside the biometrics and
  never recited back.
