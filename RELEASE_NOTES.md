**QRME v0.9.0** — **no functional change to QRME in this release**: the
three products are cut as one release, and the version moves so one number
keeps naming one combination of all three.

### What changed in the siblings

JIM-mini's medicine cabinet: medications in the user's own words, a day
board that knows done/due/missed with humane grace, one correctable answer
per slot, an as-needed ceiling that refuses to log past itself, and a
coach that notices a missed critical dose without ever alarming. JIM
remains not a pharmacist, and says so.

### Verification

1188 tests green, unchanged in behaviour.

prompted. Otherwise, download the installer for your OS from the assets
below.
